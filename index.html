Reading the four figure systems

Every horse page shows four numbers for each past race: Beyer and TimeformUS (higher is better) and the two sheets, Thoro-Graph and Ragozin (lower is better). They are different scales built with different methods, so they are never averaged. When they disagree, the race page says so and looks for the reason.

The systems

SystemDirectionWhat it isHow this hub uses it
Beyer (DRF)HigherFinal-time speed figure adjusted for track speed and distance. No adjustment for ground lost or weight. Each race shows the DRF Beyer par.Common language; compared with the race par.
TimeformUSHigherSpeed figure plus separate pace ratings. "Running Style" reads as early rating · style label · late rating (e.g., 93 Midpack 125). The PPs also give a race par, a Pace Projector and Power Picks.Early/Late ratings drive the pace maps; the par sets the bar.
Thoro-GraphLowerSheet figure in quarter points; builds in ground loss and weight. The Thoro-Pattern block gives historical odds of a new top, a pair, an off race, or a big regression (X).Main form-cycle tool; pattern %s shown on each horse.
RagozinLowerSheet figure; also builds in ground loss and weight. The overlay in the supplied PDFs shows whole numbers.Second opinion on the sheets.

  • TimeformUS ≈ Beyer + 22 on dirt (r ≈ .93) and + 23 on turf.
  • Thoro-Graph ≈ 35.1 − 0.34 × Beyer, so one TG point is worth about three Beyer points.
  • Ragozin ≈ Thoro-Graph + 5.7 on dirt and + 4.7 on turf (r ≈ .89 / .94).
  • A horse whose sheet figure is clearly better than its Beyer implies (more than about 1½ TG points) usually lost ground or carried weight. That's a hidden-form flag.

Notes on the supplied files

  • Thoro-Graph overlay: a leading "-" in front of a turf figure is the turf symbol, not a minus sign. True negatives are shown as "−". A few big races (e.g., the Whitney, Ogden Phipps) have no figure in the overlay; where I quote one, it comes from the horse's Thoro-Pattern line, and the race page says so.
  • Thoro-Pattern: T% = new top, Pr% = pairs the top, Off% = runs a bit worse, X% = big regression. "Sts" is the number of historical horses in the sample. Samples for 2-year-olds and foreign horses are often tiny; discount them.
  • TG race shapes (project doc): each horse gets a time in seconds to the first call. I use it as an early-speed indicator (lower = faster). The doc doesn't define it, so treat that reading as my interpretation.
  • TimeformUS suffix letters (t, p, f, n and others after a figure) aren't explained in the supplied PDFs; I ignore them.
  • International form: European and Japanese races have no Beyer or TimeformUS speed figure. The Timeform rating (TFR) from the PPs and any Thoro-Graph number are shown instead; the two aren't on the same scale.

Odds math

Each race's win probabilities are my estimates and add to 100% across the starters. Fair odds = 1 ÷ probability − 1 (25% → 3-1). Bet at is the price that gives a 25% cushion over fair, 1.25 ÷ probability − 1, to cover estimate error and the takeout. Morning-line odds are the track's guess, not a real price, so the "at ML" tags only tell you where to look; compare against live odds before betting.

When the evidence conflicts

  1. Check whether the race was at a comparable distance and surface.
  2. Compare sheet vs. Beyer: a gap usually means ground loss or weight.
  3. Read the trip comment and pace position.
  4. Weigh recency against the best figure: is the top repeatable (pairs, pattern %), or a one-off?
  5. Only then apply history and trainer/jockey context.

The track

Dirt
1 1/16-mile oval; 1,236-ft stretch; chutes for 4½ and 7 furlongs.
Turf (Haggin course)
7½ furlongs; 1,190-ft stretch. At a mile the run to the first turn is short, so inside-to-middle draws help horses who want position. The 1½-mile Turf has a long run to the first turn.

Source: Keeneland general information guide.

Earlier Breeders' Cups at Keeneland (2015, 2020, 2022)

  • Classic: American Pharoah (2015), Authentic (2020) and Flightline (2022, 0.44-1) were all 1st or 2nd at every call.
  • Mile: Tepin (2015) is the only winner since 2009 who raced within three lengths of the early lead. Order of Australia won at 73.20-1 in 2020; Modern Games won in 2022.
  • Turf: Found (2015) is the only 3-year-old filly to win the Turf, for Aidan O'Brien.
  • Sprint: Runhappy (2015, track record), Whitmore (2020, 16-1), Elite Power (2022, 5.55-1).
  • Turf Sprint: Caravel won at 42.89-1 in 2022.
  • Dirt Mile: Cody's Wish won at 2.16-1 in 2022.
  • Juvenile Turf: Hit It a Bomb won from post 14 in 2015.
  • Trainers: Brad Cox won four races at the 2020 Breeders' Cup.
